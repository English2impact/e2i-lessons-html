Pediatric Constipation Management

Hard stools twice a week, tears on the toilet, and a child who hides when he needs to go. This session gives you the words to explain the cycle of pain and holding on, and a set of chunks that takes a worried parent from the first question to the safety net.

ClinicIntermediate–AdvancedMedical English90 minutes

Key chunkIncreasing his daily fluid intake and introducing fibre-rich fruits like papaya or prunes will help soften his stool.

By the end you can

01Take a constipation history and spot the signs: withholding behaviour, soiling, straining and the blood of an anal fissure.
02Take a parent through a whole consultation with the chunks: probe, explain the cycle, reassure, home plan and safety net.
03Give a clear home plan (more fluids, dietary fibre, a regular toilet time, a stool softener) and the red flags that mean the child comes back straight away.
